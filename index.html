About me

I build for the problems around me.

I'm Pascal Mutaka, a full stack software engineer based in Windhoek, Namibia.

Most of my work starts with something ordinary that should be easier: sending money across borders, getting a parcel delivered, proving a training certificate is real, waiting for a haircut without losing your afternoon, or helping a child hear their own language in a game.

I like the whole journey. I model the data, write the rules, design the screens and put the product online. I care about clean architecture and careful UX, and I keep learning new tools because each project asks for something different.

#01 Delivery platform Live

Rider Africa

One website to send a parcel, book a ride, shop for groceries or pawn an item, with a single account and cart. Once an order is paid, a driver is assigned and the customer follows the delivery on a live map.

  • Separate secure logins for customers, drivers and admins
  • Card payments through DPO, plus bank transfer with proof of payment
  • Live driver location on a map, from checkout to the door
  • Next.js
  • TypeScript
  • Firebase
  • Leaflet
  • DPO Group

#04 Live queue system Live

Smart Cut Barbershop

A first come, first served queue for a barbershop. Customers reserve a place, check in on arrival, step away for a moment and watch their position update, while the barber runs the queue from the same app.

  • One place per person, even across several name-only sign-ups
  • Available in English, French and Portuguese

Workflow

How I take a project from idea to live.

  1. #01

    Understand

    I start with who uses it and what goes wrong for them today.

  2. #02

    Model the rules

    I design the data and business rules first, so the system stays correct as it grows.

  3. #03

    Build in steps

    I ship small working pieces, test them and adjust from real feedback.

  4. #04

    Launch and improve

    I deploy, watch how it behaves and keep making it better.

First, solve the problem. Then, write the code.
